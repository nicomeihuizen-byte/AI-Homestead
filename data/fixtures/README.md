# Fixtures

`thun_validation.json` is **gitignored on purpose**. It is hand-keyed from one purchased printed
edition of the Maria Thun Aussaattage for private verification of our own implementation.

Published calendars are copyrighted compilations and, in the EU, additionally protected by the
sui generis database right (Dir. 96/9/EC). The astronomical dates are facts and we compute them
ourselves; a machine-readable Thun calendar is not ours to redistribute.

Format:

```json
[
  {"date": "2026-09-01", "day_type": "leaf", "blanked": false, "source": "Aussaattage 2026, p.??"}
]
```

Target: 60–90 consecutive days spanning at least two full sidereal months.
